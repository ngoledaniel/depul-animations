About

A young creative studio focused on turning concepts into polished visual experiences.

I'm DEPUL, an animator and visual creative currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Animation at Lira University, Lira, Uganda. My work combines animation, design, photography and storytelling to create engaging visual experiences for people, brands, organizations and events.

My academic journey in Computer Animation continues to strengthen my skills in 2D animation, character design, motion graphics and digital storytelling. Through DEPUL ANIMATIONS, I am building a creative practice focused on quality, originality and attention to detail.

My goal is simple: turn ideas into visual stories that people can see, feel and remember.
